# ⛳ Clippd Platform – Contextual Analysis

---

# 🌐 Platform Overview

**:contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}** is a **golf performance analytics platform** that leverages **data science and AI** to help golfers understand, measure, and improve their game.

It transforms raw gameplay data into **actionable insights**, enabling both amateur and professional golfers to optimize performance.

---

# 🎯 Purpose & Strategic Role

Clippd exists to address a key gap in sports performance:

- Golfers often lack **clear, data-driven feedback**
- Traditional coaching is subjective and inconsistent
- Performance tracking across rounds is fragmented

### Core Objective:

To provide a **unified intelligence layer for golf performance**, combining:

- Data collection
- AI-driven analysis
- Personalized improvement recommendations

- Compares player performance against:

  - Peer groups

## 3. AI-Powered Insights

- Generates:
  - Improvement suggestions
  - Performance trends

## 4. Shot-Level Data Tracking

- Fine-grained tracking:
  - Tee shots
  - Approach shots
  - Putting

## 5. Strokes Gained Analysis

- Uses advanced golf analytics methodology:
  - Measures efficiency per shot

## 7. Player Progress Tracking
