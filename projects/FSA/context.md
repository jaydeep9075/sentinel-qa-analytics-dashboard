# 1. FSA STORE

## 🌐 Platform Overview

**:contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}** is a specialized digital commerce platform that exclusively offers products eligible under **Flexible Spending Accounts (FSA)**. It combines regulated healthcare purchasing with a guided consumer experience.

---

## 🎯 Purpose & Strategic Role

FSA Store exists to solve a critical friction in the U.S. healthcare benefits ecosystem:

- Enables users to **utilize pre-tax FSA funds efficiently**
- Reduces ambiguity around **IRS eligibility rules**
- Prevents forfeiture of unused FSA balances
- Acts as a **compliance-safe marketplace for healthcare spending**

It bridges the gap between **financial benefits systems** and **consumer retail behavior**.

---

## 🧩 Core Functional Capabilities

### Eligibility-Driven Commerce

- Entire catalog is **pre-validated for FSA compliance**
- Eliminates user-side eligibility verification

### Structured E-commerce System

- Product discovery → cart → checkout → fulfillment
- Supports promotions, bundles, and subscriptions

### FSA Perks® Ecosystem

- Loyalty rewards program
- Points accumulation and redemption
- Behavioral incentivization for repeat purchases

### Educational Infrastructure

- Eligibility List® (authoritative reference)
- Learning Center (articles, guides, compliance education)
- Financial tools (spending calculators)

### Telehealth Integration Layer

- Embedded access to healthcare services:
  - Mental health
  - Dermatology
  - Preventive care
- Hybrid commerce + service model

### LMN (Letter of Medical Necessity) Workflow

- Supports conditional eligibility products
- Guides users through documentation requirements

---

## 🗂️ Information Architecture & Site Sections

### Global Navigation Layer

- Header: Logo, search, account, cart
- Mega menu for category exploration

### Primary Sections

#### Product Discovery

- Best Sellers
- New Arrivals
- Bundles
- Trending Products
- Clearance
- Price-based filtering

#### Category Taxonomy

- Personal Care
- Medicine & Treatments
- Pain Relief & Recovery
- Baby & Mom
- Home Health Care
- Condition-based navigation

#### Telehealth Services

- Men's health
- Women's health
- Mental wellness
- Virtual consultations

#### Knowledge & Resources

- Eligibility List®
- Learning Center
- Savings tools

---

## 🧠 UX/UI Characteristics

- Commerce-first layout with **compliance overlays**
- High use of:
  - Banners (deadlines, urgency messaging)
  - Category-driven navigation
- Mobile-responsive, accessibility-aware design
- Decision-support UX (filters, eligibility clarity)

---

## ⚙️ Technical Architecture (Inferred)

- Likely built on **Salesforce Commerce Cloud**
- Integrated systems:
  - Payment gateways
  - Telehealth APIs
  - Rewards/loyalty engines
- Heavy use of client-side rendering (JavaScript)

---

## 🧪 QA & Testing Focus Areas

- Eligibility tagging accuracy
- Checkout/payment flows
- Rewards system calculations
- LMN validation workflows
- External service integrations
- Navigation + search reliability

# 3. WELLDESERVED HEALTH

## 🌐 Platform Overview

**:contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}** is a **benefits-driven digital health platform** focused on connecting users with **personalized healthcare services and wellness solutions**, often in partnership with employers or benefit providers.

---

## 🎯 Purpose & Strategic Role

WellDeserved Health operates at a different layer compared to FSA/HSA stores:

- Focuses on **health engagement rather than direct commerce**
- Enables users to **access and activate healthcare benefits**
- Promotes **preventive care and holistic wellness**
- Acts as a **gateway between users and healthcare services**

---

## 🧩 Core Functional Capabilities

### Benefits Activation Platform

- Centralized access to healthcare programs
- Employer-sponsored benefit integration

### Service Marketplace (Non-traditional Commerce)

- Access to:
  - Mental health programs
  - Chronic condition management
  - Wellness coaching
- Focus on **services over products**

### Personalization Engine

- Tailored recommendations
- User-specific health journeys

### Engagement & Guidance Layer

- Guided onboarding
- Program discovery
- Health education

### Partner Ecosystem Integration

- Connects with third-party providers
- Aggregates multiple healthcare services into one interface

---

## 🗂️ Information Architecture & Site Sections

# 🧠 Final Context for AI Systems

- **FSA Store and HSA Store** represent:
  - Transactional healthcare commerce systems
  - Strong compliance + eligibility-driven design
  - Retail + fintech hybrid platforms

- **WellDeserved Health** represents:
  - Engagement and orchestration layer
  - Service aggregation ecosystem
  - User-centric healthcare journey platform

### Key Insight:

Together, these platforms illustrate a **full-stack digital health ecosystem**:

- Spending (FSA/HSA)
- Tracking (HSA)
- Engagement & care delivery (WellDeserved Health)
