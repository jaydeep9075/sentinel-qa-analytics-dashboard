# TR-Insight — One-Pager

**AI QE that knows your results.**

## The elevator pitch

Your team already produces test results. TR-Insight turns them into answers anyone can get in seconds — ask a question in plain English and receive the summary, the chart, and a clear release call. No manual reporting, no waiting on the one person who knows where the data lives.

## The problem

- A release is hours away and nobody can say, with evidence, whether it's safe to ship.
- Every status update starts with someone exporting results into a spreadsheet by hand.
- The same failure means one thing to leadership and something else to the engineer who owns the suite.
- Flaky tests hide inside the noise until they take down a release.
- Insight depends on the one person who knows where the data lives — and they're on leave.

## What changes with TR-Insight

- Ask in plain English and get the answer, the chart and the caveat together.
- Summaries and visuals are produced on demand — no weekly reporting chore.
- One set of results, role-aware answers: executive risk framing for leadership, full engineering detail for the team that owns the suite.
- Regressions, flaky tests and recurring patterns are surfaced automatically across builds.
- Anyone with a login can self-serve, so analysis stops being a bottleneck.

## Who it's for

| Role | The question they open the dashboard to answer | What they get |
|---|---|---|
| **CTO** | "Are we safe to ship?" | Release risk and quality trend in plain language — a defensible go/no-go in one screen, no deck required |
| **QA Lead** | "Where is quality actually weak?" | The modules dragging the pass rate down, ranked by impact — effort goes where it changes the number |
| **Test Manager** | "What do I report this week?" | Coverage, pass rate and regressions across builds, on demand — reporting time collapses to a question |
| **SDET** | "Why did this fail?" | The failing tests, their errors, the flaky ones and the slowest ones — straight to what's worth debugging |

## What you can do

- **Ask in plain English** — "is the payment module stable?" — and get a direct, sourced answer.
- **Charts and dashboards on demand** — presentation-ready visuals, ready to drop into a status update or a steering call.
- **Automatic trends and patterns** — recurring failures, regressions and flaky tests found, explained and ranked, with a recommendation on what to fix first.
- **Answers shaped for the reader** — leadership gets risk and release framing, engineers get failing tests and detail, from one source of truth.
- **A go/no-go you can defend** — a clear release-readiness verdict, with the failing evidence one click away when someone challenges it.
- **Visibility while it's running** — follow a test run as it happens, so problems surface during execution instead of in tomorrow's report.

## Bring the data you already have

Automation reports, spreadsheet exports, Excel workbooks maintained by hand, existing databases, internal reporting services, and live test runs — connected in a few clicks. No migration project, no engineering effort, nothing to rebuild.

## Why AI belongs here

- Test data is large, repetitive and dull to read — exactly the work people postpone and AI does instantly.
- It reads every result, not the sample someone had time to look at, so nothing quietly gets skipped.
- It explains what happened in language a non-technical stakeholder can act on, with no translation layer.
- It ranks what matters — the failure blocking the release ahead of the twenty that don't.

## AI you can budget for

- Only the information a question actually needs reaches the model — never the entire dataset.
- Repeat and follow-up questions reuse work already done instead of paying for it twice.
- Every user and team has a spend limit, so cost can't run away between invoices.
- Usage is visible per person and per project, so what AI costs — and why — is never a surprise.

## Your data stays yours

TR-Insight runs inside your own environment. Release information, defect detail and customer-sensitive test data never leave your infrastructure — which keeps security review short and procurement simple. A single Docker Compose command brings the whole thing up.

## The outcome

- **Hours back every week** — the export-and-summarize cycle disappears; questions that used to take an afternoon are answered while the meeting is still running.
- **Decisions you can defend** — every answer carries the data it came from, so release calls stop being opinion.
- **Problems caught earlier** — regressions and unstable areas surface while a fix is still cheap, not after they've cost a release.
- **AI cost you can predict** — spend is capped per person and per team, a known line item rather than an open-ended bill.
